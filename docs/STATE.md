# Current state

## Active direction

The production direction remains Treatment A, “The market opens.” The stand's top beam, side posts, hardware, bottom fascia, and counter are now the permanent architecture before, during, and after the opening. This supersedes the former foreground-table exit. The rejected Week-at-the-Stand experiment is not part of this repository or design. Browser captures and deployment do not constitute permanent artistic approval.

## Repository and publication policy

This is the existing public `Denoax/farm-stand` worktree and deploys through its existing GitHub Pages workflow to `https://denoax.github.io/farm-stand/`.

Standing publication authorization covers a coherent, inspected, tested integration through the existing main branch and Pages workflow. It does not enable real orders, payments, bookings, message delivery, analytics, purchases, credential changes, force-pushes, destructive history changes, or unrelated infrastructure.

## Current implementation

The 6.5-second opening is time-controlled after one intentional downward wheel, touch, or appropriate page-navigation key. One owner provides a one-second initial scroll hold after scene readiness; it releases on its independent deadline and on Escape, navigation/control activation, touch cancellation, page hiding, reduced-motion change, failure, or unmount. Returning sessions and direct hashes bypass it.

The live threshold has one permanent timber frame, fixed steel shutter rails, one monotonic rising shutter, and the retained CC0 apple. Its desktop and portrait rear plates were generated against captures of the actual frame/camera guides, then inspected in the integrated scene. The old broad descending passage, duplicate foreground table, and unlit oval shadow are removed. The irregular apple is centered from its real bounds; a sampled support envelope keeps it on the counter while distance determines rotation, and the short roll ends behind the left post. After an uncluttered rest, the service copy and four individual photo links return and remain.

The frame uses cropped single-board derivatives of Poly Haven's CC0 Rough Wood diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps. Grain direction follows each member, board variants avoid obvious repetition, and the top beam/counter remain registered at portrait, desktop, and wide aspect ratios. The photo cluster moves inward on wide layouts and each paper rotates around a deliberately irregular weathered-steel nail head.

The visible brand is one original open-barn SVG master reused through one Logo component in the header and footer and directly as the favicon. Header navigation remains HTML set in the locally delivered Source Sans 3 face. Warm olive action tokens are shared by principal commerce actions and enabled quantity controls.

The 48-product catalogue, all department counts, real photographs, three licensed farm-life videos, variants, integer money, basket reducer, quantity bounds, persistence migration, collection-preview semantics, direct hashes, and fictional transaction boundaries remain intact. Basket persistence stores validated identifiers and bounded integer quantities; displayed prices come from the current catalogue.

The drawer uses one explicit row grid with adjacent thumbnail/copy, aligned totals, and a deliberate quantity/remove row. Quantity controls are fixed 44×44 circles. The superseded disclaimer and implementation-oriented saved-price sentence are absent from the customer-facing drawer.

The opening has no visible Pause/Resume control. Escape, direct navigation, page hiding, reduced-motion changes, and scene failure still settle or bypass the opening; header and in-scene destination links remain accessible throughout. The copy's departure is an 850 ms opacity-led fade contained inside the bounded 900 ms initial scroll hold.

## Static and failure states

The first-paint and settled posters are deterministic captures of the same implemented frame, camera, materials, light, and plate used by the live scene. Model/WebGL failure, delayed loading, direct destinations, and reduced motion therefore retain a complete permanent-frame composition rather than revealing an empty aperture. Opening-image failure uses the existing farm-setting fallback with live HTML. The scene stops rendering offscreen and settles permanently after the opening.

## Validation and evidence

Bulky local captures are ignored under `evidence/permanent-frame/`; the uploadable review archive is produced separately and is not deployed. `docs/OPENING-UI-FINISH-REVIEW.md` remains historical evidence for the superseded source state.

## Known limitations

- Automated captures use software-rendered Chromium; physical-phone, hardware-GPU, Safari, screen-reader, and independent human art-direction reviews remain outstanding.
- With no visible pause button, touch users can stop the short automatic sequence by choosing a destination but do not have a dedicated on-screen pause affordance; reduced-motion users bypass it entirely.
- The generated rear plates depict a fictional property and sample produce, not verified inventory or a real farm.
- The lazy Three.js chunk remains above Vite’s generic 500 kB raw advisory.
- Public identity details, real contact delivery, commerce backends, and analytics remain intentionally unconfigured.
