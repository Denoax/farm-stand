## Known limitations

- Automated captures use software-rendered Chromium; physical-phone, hardware-GPU, Safari, screen-reader, and independent human art-direction reviews remain outstanding.
- With no visible pause button, touch users can stop the short automatic sequence by choosing a destination but do not have a dedicated on-screen pause affordance; reduced-motion users bypass it entirely.
- The Mark Stebnicki orchard is licensed illustrative scenery, not the user’s property, an Alberta client, or evidence of live inventory.
- The two exact user-supplied leaf movies are locally delivered as silent alpha VP9 derivatives. Transition 01 maps the stand to the shop; Transition 02 maps only hero animal-Polaroid activation. Source hashes, key method, crop and decoded opaque intervals are recorded in `assets/manifest.json`.
- Bird collision/support diagnostics derive from the skinned mesh envelope and named ankle/toe bones in software-rendered Chromium; they are engineering evidence, not a claim of real-world physics or universal device rendering.
- Automated audio checks prove attempted startup, honest blocked state, interaction retry, semantic routing, file-failure handling, cooldown and an actual captured application audio track; they do not establish subjective headphone/speaker mix approval. Browser policy can block automatic playback, and the interface does not claim otherwise.
- The paired software-rendered benchmark retained the same approximately 83.3 ms median frame interval while reducing draw calls from 33 to 30 and decoded material bytes from 611 KB to 529 KB. Candidate tail intervals were less stable (p95 183–200 ms with one 250 ms maximum versus a 183 ms baseline p95), so this is not claimed as a performance improvement or physical-device result.
- The separately described original sketch sheet and transparent crop masters were absent. The public pencil derivatives use exact pixels from the supplied preview composite with paper-colour transparency; they are faithful preview derivatives, not independently verified original-master crops.
- The lazy Three.js chunk remains above Vite’s generic 500 kB raw advisory.
- Public identity details, real contact delivery, commerce backends, and analytics remain intentionally unconfigured.
