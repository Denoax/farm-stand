## Current implementation

The 6.5-second opening is time-controlled after one intentional downward wheel, touch, or appropriate page-navigation key. One owner provides a single temporary document-scroll gate after scene readiness; normal release occurs only when the renderer reports the complete presented frame. A separate nine-second fail-open watchdog covers stalled presentation. Escape, navigation/control activation, touch cancellation, page hiding, reduced-motion change, focus departure, failure, or unmount settle the decoration and release immediately. Returning sessions and direct hashes bypass it, and the gate never reacquires.

The live threshold has one permanent timber frame, fixed steel shutter rails, one monotonic rising shutter, and the retained CC0 apple. Mark Stebnicki's licensed bright orchard photograph supplies separate desktop and deliberate portrait crops selected inside the actual aperture. The former three-board photo backing wall is removed; the permanent structural frame and measured apple occlusion stay intact. The irregular apple is centered from its real bounds; a sampled support envelope keeps it on the counter while distance determines rotation. Its fixed rear lane retains measured depth clearance behind the left post and ends fully occluded. After an uncluttered rest, the service copy and four enlarged individual photo links return over the orchard and remain.

The frame uses cropped single-board derivatives of Poly Haven's CC0 Rough Wood diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps. Grain direction follows each member, board variants avoid obvious repetition, and the top beam/counter remain registered at portrait, desktop, and wide aspect ratios. The four photo anchors are roughly 10–15% larger, move inward on wide layouts, use locally delivered Caveat captions, and rotate around deliberately irregular weathered-steel nail heads.

The first continued movement into the shop after a played opening triggers one explicit leaf state machine: `idle → armed → entering → covered → clearing → complete`. Four edge panels built from the retained real-leaf source close for 600 ms, hold complete opaque leaf coverage for about one second while the shop destination commits underneath, and clear for 800 ms. Escape, other navigation, visibility loss, failure, direct entry, and reduced motion bypass safely. The floating basket stays hidden/inert until the handoff completes or a bypass is established.

Music is explicitly opt-in, starts off, and is represented by one icon-only Music toggle. Shutter, leaf, semantic add/details/quantity/remove feedback, and cooldown-bound animal cues are independent of the music state after a browser-permitted activation. The shutter joins an in-progress lift at the corresponding buffer offset and is discarded after the lift rather than replayed stale. Cattle gain is reduced from `0.42` to `0.167`. Visibility and teardown stop/suspend playback; rejected permission or a missing file leaves the visual application fully usable. Existing farm-life videos remain muted.

The visible brand is one original open-barn SVG master reused through one Logo component in the header and footer and directly as the favicon. Header navigation remains HTML set in the locally delivered Source Sans 3 face. Warm olive action tokens are shared by principal commerce actions and enabled quantity controls.

The 48-product catalogue, all department counts, real photographs, three licensed farm-life videos, variants, integer money, basket reducer, quantity bounds, persistence migration, collection-preview semantics, direct hashes, and fictional transaction boundaries remain intact. One fixed bottom-right trigger and click-open mini-basket both derive from that original reducer; no second cart state exists. The opening has no visible Pause/Resume control. Escape, direct navigation, page hiding, reduced-motion changes, and scene failure still settle or bypass the opening; header and in-scene destination links remain accessible throughout. The copy's departure is an 850 ms opacity-led fade inside the single completion-bound hold. The two explicitly removed hero passages are absent.

## Static and failure states

The first-paint and settled posters are deterministic captures of the same implemented frame, camera, materials, lighting, and bright orchard plate used by the live scene. Model/WebGL failure, delayed loading, plate failure, direct destinations, and reduced motion therefore retain a complete permanent-frame composition rather than revealing an empty or unrelated aperture. The scene stops rendering offscreen and settles permanently after the opening.

## Known limitations

- Automated captures use software-rendered Chromium; physical-phone, hardware-GPU, Safari, screen-reader, and independent human art-direction reviews remain outstanding.
- With no visible pause button, touch users can stop the short automatic sequence by choosing a destination but do not have a dedicated on-screen pause affordance; reduced-motion users bypass it entirely.
- The Mark Stebnicki orchard is licensed illustrative scenery, not the user’s property, an Alberta client, or evidence of live inventory.
- The supplied leaf reference was visually played and informed the perimeter-to-centre leaf curtain. The supplied shutter reference page was played and identified, but this environment could not provide reliable subjective audio audition; its soundtrack was not copied because no reuse grant was established.
- Automated audio checks prove activation, semantic routing, file-failure handling, cooldown and an actual captured application audio track; they do not establish subjective headphone/speaker mix approval. Browser policy can still block an untrusted first-wheel shutter, and the interface does not claim otherwise.
- The lazy Three.js chunk remains above Vite’s generic 500 kB raw advisory.
- Public identity details, real contact delivery, commerce backends, and analytics remain intentionally unconfigured.
