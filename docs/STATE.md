## Current implementation

The 6.5-second opening is time-controlled after one intentional downward wheel, touch, or appropriate page-navigation key. One owner-aware document-scroll gate serves both the opening and leaf handoff without overlapping locks. Opening release occurs only when the renderer reports the complete presented frame; the leaf owner retains the same lock through cover, hidden destination commit, and clear. Separate single-shot nine-second opening and 3.6-second leaf watchdogs fail open. Escape, navigation/control activation, touch cancellation, page hiding, reduced-motion change, focus departure, failure, or unmount settle or bypass the decoration and release immediately. Returning sessions and direct hashes bypass it, and neither phase reacquires after completion.

## Static and failure states

The first-paint and settled posters are deterministic captures of the same implemented frame, camera, materials, lighting, and bright orchard plate used by the live scene. Model/WebGL failure, delayed loading, plate failure, direct destinations, and reduced motion therefore retain a complete permanent-frame composition rather than revealing an empty or unrelated aperture. The scene stops rendering offscreen and settles permanently after the opening.

## Known limitations

- Automated captures use software-rendered Chromium; physical-phone, hardware-GPU, Safari, screen-reader, and independent human art-direction reviews remain outstanding.
- With no visible pause button, touch users can stop the short automatic sequence by choosing a destination but do not have a dedicated on-screen pause affordance; reduced-motion users bypass it entirely.
- The Mark Stebnicki orchard is licensed illustrative scenery, not the user’s property, an Alberta client, or evidence of live inventory.
- The supplied leaf reference was visually played and informed the perimeter-to-centre leaf curtain. The supplied shutter reference page was played and identified, but this environment could not provide reliable subjective audio audition; its soundtrack was not copied because no reuse grant was established.
- Automated audio checks prove attempted startup, honest blocked state, interaction retry, semantic routing, file-failure handling, cooldown and an actual captured application audio track; they do not establish subjective headphone/speaker mix approval. Browser policy can block automatic playback, and the interface does not claim otherwise.
- The lazy Three.js chunk remains above Vite’s generic 500 kB raw advisory.
- Public identity details, real contact delivery, commerce backends, and analytics remain intentionally unconfigured.
