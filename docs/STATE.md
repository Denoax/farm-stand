## Current implementation

The 6.5-second opening is time-controlled after one intentional downward wheel, touch, or appropriate page-navigation key. One owner provides a single temporary document-scroll gate after scene readiness; normal release occurs only when the renderer reports the complete presented frame. A separate nine-second fail-open watchdog covers stalled presentation. Escape, navigation/control activation, touch cancellation, page hiding, reduced-motion change, focus departure, failure, or unmount settle the decoration and release immediately. Returning sessions and direct hashes bypass it, and the gate never reacquires.

The live threshold has one permanent timber frame, fixed steel shutter rails, one monotonic rising shutter, and the retained CC0 apple. A licensed real photograph by Hudson Graves supplies the desktop and deliberately cropped portrait farm view; a three-board timber reveal from the live material system supports the photo cluster. The old broad descending passage, duplicate foreground table, and unlit oval shadow are removed. The irregular apple is centered from its real bounds; a sampled support envelope keeps it on the counter while distance determines rotation. Its fixed rear lane retains measured depth clearance behind the left post and ends fully occluded. After an uncluttered rest, the service copy and four individual photo links return and remain.

The frame uses cropped single-board derivatives of Poly Haven's CC0 Rough Wood diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps. Grain direction follows each member, board variants avoid obvious repetition, and the top beam/counter remain registered at portrait, desktop, and wide aspect ratios. The four photo anchors are roughly 10–15% larger, move inward on wide layouts, use locally delivered Caveat captions, and rotate around deliberately irregular weathered-steel nail heads.

The first continued movement into the shop after a played opening triggers one bounded 1.58-second leaf handoff using deterministic real-leaf sprites. It never extends the opening gate, does not intercept input, stays behind the persistent header and sound controls, cleans itself up, does not replay, and is omitted for reduced motion or direct entry.

Sound is explicitly opt-in and initially makes no audio requests. One shared Web Audio owner provides a global Sound on/off control, independent music on/off and volume, a low-level music bed, presented-phase shutter cue, restrained interface feedback, leaf rustle, and cooldown-bound animal cues. Mute stops all voices; visibility and teardown stop/suspend playback; missing or rejected audio leaves the visual application fully usable. Existing farm-life videos remain muted.

The visible brand is one original open-barn SVG master reused through one Logo component in the header and footer and directly as the favicon. Header navigation remains HTML set in the locally delivered Source Sans 3 face. Warm olive action tokens are shared by principal commerce actions and enabled quantity controls.

The 48-product catalogue, all department counts, real photographs, three licensed farm-life videos, variants, integer money, basket reducer, quantity bounds, persistence migration, collection-preview semantics, direct hashes, and fictional transaction boundaries remain intact. Basket persistence stores validated identifiers and bounded integer quantities; displayed prices come from the current catalogue.

The drawer uses one explicit row grid with adjacent thumbnail/copy, aligned totals, and a deliberate quantity/remove row. Quantity controls are fixed 44×44 circles. The superseded disclaimer and implementation-oriented saved-price sentence are absent from the customer-facing drawer.

The opening has no visible Pause/Resume control. Escape, direct navigation, page hiding, reduced-motion changes, and scene failure still settle or bypass the opening; header and in-scene destination links remain accessible throughout. The copy's departure is an 850 ms opacity-led fade inside the single completion-bound hold. The two explicitly removed hero passages are absent.

## Static and failure states

The first-paint and settled posters are deterministic captures of the same implemented frame, camera, materials, light, timber reveal, and real-farm plate used by the live scene. Model/WebGL failure, delayed loading, plate failure, direct destinations, and reduced motion therefore retain a complete permanent-frame composition rather than revealing an empty or unrelated aperture. The scene stops rendering offscreen and settles permanently after the opening.

## Known limitations

- Automated captures use software-rendered Chromium; physical-phone, hardware-GPU, Safari, screen-reader, and independent human art-direction reviews remain outstanding.
- With no visible pause button, touch users can stop the short automatic sequence by choosing a destination but do not have a dedicated on-screen pause affordance; reduced-motion users bypass it entirely.
- The Hudson Graves photograph depicts a real Leicester, New York farm but is illustrative third-party scenery, not the user’s property, an Alberta client, or evidence of live inventory.
- The supplied leaf reference was visually played and informed the perimeter-to-centre leaf curtain. The supplied shutter reference page was played and identified, but this environment could not provide reliable subjective audio audition; its soundtrack was not copied because no reuse grant was established.
- Automated audio checks prove activation, routing, file-failure handling, cooldown and an actual captured application audio track; they do not establish subjective headphone/speaker mix approval.
- The lazy Three.js chunk remains above Vite’s generic 500 kB raw advisory.
- Public identity details, real contact delivery, commerce backends, and analytics remain intentionally unconfigured.
