# Farm-stand art workflow

Read `HANDOFF.md` and relevant current state. This skill is project-specific, not a generic cinematic landing-page template.

## 1. Identify the visual job

Name the dominant subject and useful visitor action in one sentence. The promise is a farm stand that becomes a usable website example. The actual offer remains website services. No speculative farm simulator or fake produce business.

## 2. Inspect ingredients before choreography

Use real source images/models. Verify that the downloaded maps and geometry reproduce the intended material. Compare at intended screen size under the proposed light; a catalogue thumbnail is not proof. Maintain source and derivative records. Do not substitute a procedural fruit or unavailable asset without stating it.

Background plates, simple structural geometry and live produce models may coexist. Their perspective, scale, light, colour and contact must agree. Inspect alpha cutouts and underlying clean plates where applicable. Prefer a smaller excellent scene to more objects.

## 3. Compose the still first

Establish a clear focal product, a believable table/crate relationship, restrained background detail and a readable text area. Inspect desktop and portrait. Keep useful produce large enough to appreciate. Do not use empty space as an excuse for weak scale or giant typography as a substitute for scenery.

Judge the frame with animation paused. Correct lighting, crop and asset choice before adding movement. The user has approved the direction, not a particular generated picture or browser baseline.

## 4. Author one meaningful transition

The same produce remains visible while the physical setting becomes a usable product demonstration. One dominant movement at a time; gentle camera/depth change is sufficient. Keep object contact credible. No flythrough, arbitrary orbit, bouncing text or repeated pinning just to consume scroll.

Do not tie product selection resets to scroll direction. HTML controls remain operable during animation. Do not hide service/contact access behind completion of the scene.

## 5. Resolve the entire page

Carry colour, material and typography into product controls, the pickup preview, service explanation and contact ending. Avoid the automatic three-feature-card footer. Make mobile a separate composition; give reduced motion a complete, shorter/static version.

## 6. Inspect, correct, continue

Use actual browser pixels and a forward/reverse traversal. Fix the most important visual defect, not the easiest measurable detail. Prefer an asset substitution when an inadequate asset is the cause. Preserve good work and continue to the integrated local review; do not ask the user to approve every internal edit.

Do not equate generated imagery, a technical pass, or a screenshot's existence with user approval. Source selection, artistic judgment and user acceptance remain separate.
